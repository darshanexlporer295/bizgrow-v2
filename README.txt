BizGrow demo - read me first

WHAT IT IS
A demo of a shop app. The shop, the people and the sales are examples.

HOW TO OPEN IT
1. Unzip the file.
2. Open v2/dist/bizgrow-v2.html in Chrome or Edge on your computer or phone.
3. On the first screen, tap "Sign in". Use owner@example.com and the password example-pass-123. (Any password of 8 or more letters also works in the demo.)

IMPORTANT
- This is a demo only. Your data stays in this browser.
- There are no real accounts and no server yet.
- Clearing the browser's site data deletes the demo data.

THREE THINGS TO TRY FIRST
1. On the Home screen, type "2 kg atta 480 rupees" in the box and tap Check. Read the sheet, then tap Save.
2. Open Udhar (credit list) and look at the days late and the reminder link.
3. Open Team, add a person, then suspend or remove them.

FOLDERS
- original/ : the untouched copy of the app.
- v2/ : the new version. v2/dist/bizgrow-v2.html is the single file to open.
- CHANGES.md : what changed, what was tested, and what is still missing.
